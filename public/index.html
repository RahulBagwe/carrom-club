Carrom Rules & Modes

Game Modes:
• vs Bot / 2P Pass & Play / 4P Doubles: In 4P Doubles, partners sit opposite each other (P1 & P3 vs. P2 & P4) and turns rotate counter-clockwise around all 4 sides.
• ICF Official: 25-pt / 8-board / Best-of-3 match rules, alternating break, 22-pt Queen cutoff, and interactive Due placement.
• Quick 1-Board: Single-board match—first to clear all 9 coins wins.
• Freestyle: White = 10 pts, Black = 20 pts, Queen = 50 pts (cover with any coin). First to 160 pts wins.
• Trick Shots: 6 verified solo puzzle stages!

Break & Placement: Whoever breaks plays White (White Y points at them). The striker cannot overlap any coin on the baseline and must either cover a red Base Circle completely or stay clear.

Official ICF Dues & Queen Laws (Laws 56–104):
• Striker + Coins (Laws 56–60): Striker alone = 1 Due & lose turn. Striker + own coin (with or without opponent coin) = own coin(s) + 1 Due returned & turn continues.
• Queen Eligibility & Break Cover (Laws 79–92): Must have ≥1 own coin pocketed (or a recovered Due, Law 84) and 0 unrecovered Dues (Law 83) to claim Queen. If all 9 coins are on the board, pocketing Queen + 1 own coin requires covering on the next stroke; Queen + 2 own coins covers immediately (Law 88). While covering Queen, Striker + own coin returns the coin + 1 Due and continues turn to cover (Law 92).
• Last-Coin Laws (Laws 93–102): Clearing your last coin (or opponent's last coin) while Queen is unsettled loses the board (3 pts, or 1 pt at 22+), plus +1 penalty point if the Striker was also pocketed.
• Due Placement & General (Laws 30, 104, 115, 124): 15s shot/placement timer in ICF mode; Due coins cannot form a direct pocket-aligned Pair/Cannon with the Queen (Law 104); a striker slip that stays within the baselines without touching a coin is retaken (Law 115); 3 consecutive passes by each player replays the board (Law 124).

Controls: Touch/Mouse drag-back on the striker, or Keyboard (←→ slide, AD aim, ↑↓ power, Space shoot).
