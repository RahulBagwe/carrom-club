Carrom Rules & Modes

Game Modes:
• vs Bot / 2P Pass & Play / 4P Doubles: In 4P Doubles, partners sit opposite each other (P1 & P3 vs. P2 & P4) and turns rotate counter-clockwise around all 4 sides.
• ICF Official: 25-pt / 8-board / Best-of-3 match rules, alternating break, 22-pt Queen cutoff, and interactive Due placement.
• Quick 1-Board: Single-board match—first to clear all 9 coins wins.
• Freestyle: White = 10 pts, Black = 20 pts, Queen = 50 pts (cover with any coin). First to 160 pts wins.
• Trick Shots: 6 verified solo puzzle stages!

Break & Placement: Whoever breaks plays White (White Y points at them). The striker cannot overlap any coin on the baseline and must either cover a red Base Circle completely or stay clear.

Dues & Queen: Pocketing the striker incurs 1 Due. In ICF/Quick, pocket 1 own coin before claiming the Queen, cover her on the same or next stroke, and never clear your last coin while the Queen is still on the board.

Controls: Touch/Mouse drag-back on the striker, or Keyboard (←→ slide, AD aim, ↑↓ power, Space shoot).
